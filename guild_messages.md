## VERSION 2 — Full Guild (after leadership approves)

Hey everyone — sharing a free tool I built that might help you make better decisions about your city.

It ranks every FoE building by value per tile across four modes: Balanced, GBG, GE, and QI. Buildings get S/A/B/C tiers so it's easy to scan.

The best part: upload your own city export and it shows YOUR buildings specifically — which ones are strong, which are C-tier swap candidates, and how your city scores in each mode.

How to use it:
1. Open FoE Helper → City Plan → Copy City Data
2. Paste into any text editor and save the file with a .json extension
3. Upload at the link below — takes about 30 seconds, runs in your browser

geomeskin.github.io/FoE_Boost-n-Optimizer

Free, no account, nothing stored. Give it a try and let me know what you find!
