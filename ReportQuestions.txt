
Settings >> Other (tab) >> Import/Export (Menu Item) >> Open Import/Export Tool >> Scroll to bottom Export Data (button)

Open the "Town Overview" dialog (displays the schematics of your city) >> Top Right corner click "Copy city data"
on your hard drive create an empty TEXT file and name it whatever you like but give it a '.json' extension.

After you've clicked the 'copy city data' button open the new text file and PASTE from the clipboard to the file, and SAVE.

Then you can use the tool.

The Foe Boost-n-Optimizer tool.

It's a free browser tool that ranks every building in FoE by value per tile, across all four game modes (Balanced, GBG, GE, QI) — S/A/B/C tiers. You upload your own city export and it'll show you exactly which of your placed buildings are worth keeping and which are swap candidates.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere.

Only requirement is FoE Helper (to export your city).

Take a look and let me know what you think:
geomeskin.github.io/FoE_Boost-n-Optimizer/

Happy to answer questions. If you think it's worth sharing with the guild, I'll write something up.
